Chocolate Cake Recipe

A rich, moist chocolate cake that is simple to prepare and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and prepare a cake pan.
  2. Mix the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder in a large bowl.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract, then mix until smooth.
  4. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and spread it evenly.
  5. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let the cake cool before slicing and serving.
